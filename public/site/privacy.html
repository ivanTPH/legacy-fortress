ID checks and your face

An ID check compares your selfie with the photo on your ID. It happens at two moments only: when you accept an invitation to someone's Vault, and when you ask for a Vault to be opened after a death or loss of capacity. The comparison runs on our own servers; no outside ID company receives your photos. We don't keep a face template. Photos are deleted as soon as the check is decided, and in any case within 14 days. We keep only the result and its date. You can refuse, but then you can't be connected to a Vault.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download, restrict or delete your information, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent for ID checks at any time. Most of this you can do in Settings; for anything else, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We reply within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

After you die

UK data protection law covers living people, but we keep treating your Vault with the same care: it is released only to the people you chose, after evidence is reviewed by a person and their ID is checked again.
