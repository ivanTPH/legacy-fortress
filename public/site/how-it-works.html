How it works

Set up in an afternoon. Ready for the day your family needs it.

Five simple steps, done at your own pace. Here's what you'll see.

  1. Step 1

    Add what you have

    Start with whatever's easiest: a bank account, your pension, your home. Pick the type, add a few details, attach the statement or deed if you have it. Two of each kind are free.

    • checkYou choose how much detail to add
    • checkUpload a statement and we'll read the account details for you to check
    • checkNothing is shared with anyone
  2. Step 2

    Add your Will and Power of Attorney

    Upload your Will. We read it and find the people it names, such as your executors, for you to check. Do the same for a Lasting Power of Attorney.

    • checkWills, Powers of Attorney, trusts and funeral wishes in one place
    • checkNames found in the document are suggestions; nothing happens until you agree
    • checkFree on every plan
  3. Step 3

    Name the people who'll help

    Add your executors, attorneys, family and advisers. Your To do list walks you through each one: add, invite, and choose what they'll receive later.

    • checkGrouped as executors, family and advisers
    • checkSee who has joined and who hasn't
    • checkBeing added never shows anyone your records
  4. mailbadgeverified_user
    Step 4

    Invite them, safely

    Each person gets an email invitation. When they join, they prove who they are with a quick ID check: a photo of their passport or driving licence and a selfie.

    • checkThey see nothing of yours just by joining
    • checkYou choose exactly what each person can ever see
    • checkStop or change it at any time
  5. Step 5

    Released when it's needed

    After a death, your executor reports it with the death certificate. A real person on our team checks the evidence and the grant of probate before anything opens, and only what you chose is shown.

    • checkNothing opens without checks by a person
    • checkPower of Attorney can be activated if you lose capacity
    • checkYour family is never locked out because of a plan

Questions people ask

How long does it take?

Most people add the essentials in about an hour, then add more over time. You can stop and come back whenever you like.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. It works like any simple website, with large text and one step at a time. A family member or adviser can sit with you, and help is a click away.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on any computer with your email, password and a code we email you. Your Vault isn't stored on your phone.

Can my family see everything straight away?

No. They see only what you choose, and only when you choose: now, or after your death once our team has checked the evidence.
