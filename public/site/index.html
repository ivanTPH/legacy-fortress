Most people have nothing written down. Their families are left to search.

Some leave a note in a drawer; most leave nothing at all. When someone dies, the people closest to them have to work out which banks, pensions, policies and accounts there were, where the will is and who to call, while they're grieving.

Tracking down assets through a solicitor can take months and cost a great deal. Legacy Fortress solves this in one place: your financial, personal and business affairs under one roof, ready for the people you choose when they need it.

How it works

Four steps. You stay in control of every one.

  1. add_circleStep 1

    Add what matters

    Bank accounts, pensions, property, policies, wills, digital accounts and your wishes, with the documents that go with them.

  2. groupStep 2

    Name your people

    Executors, attorneys, family and advisers. They confirm who they are with an ID check before they're connected.

  3. keyStep 3

    Share only what you choose

    Everything is private by default. Share a single record with one person, add an end date, and stop it at any time.

  4. lock_openStep 4

    Released when it's needed

    After a death or loss of capacity, evidence is reviewed by a person and the ID is checked again before anything opens.

Questions people ask

Can Legacy Fortress staff see my records?

No. Our team sees only what it needs to run the service, such as whether an ID check passed. Record names, values and documents are never shown in our admin tools.

How does my family get access when I die?

The people you named report the death and upload the certificate. A person on our team reviews it, they confirm their ID again, and only then can they see what you chose for them.

Is this a will?

No. Legacy Fortress keeps your will and tells your family where it is and who to contact. A will still needs to be made and signed properly, usually with a solicitor.

What happens if I stop paying for Premium?

Nothing is deleted. You move to the free plan, everything stays readable, and your family's access after your death still works. You just can't add more than the free limits.

Can I take my information out?

Yes, on any plan. Download your records and documents whenever you like.
