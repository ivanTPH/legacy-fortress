About billing

Do I pay during the free trial?

No. You add a card when you start, nothing is taken for 14 days, and we email you 3 days before the trial ends. Cancel before then and you won't be charged.

What if I go back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. If you're over the free limits, everything stays readable and shareable; you just can't add more until you remove some or upgrade again.

What counts as a document?

One file. A ten-page will scanned as a single PDF counts once. Premium includes 100 documents, which covers most households; we'll let you know when you reach 90, and you can add 100 more for £1 a month (£12 a year on a yearly plan).

Who handles my card details?

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by Legacy Fortress.

Does my family pay to see what I've shared?

Never. People you share with, and your executors after your death, use Legacy Fortress free for everything in your Vault.
