# V5.2 Vault Access, Nominations and Conditional Release — architecture proposal

27 October 2026 · **Planning only. No schema, permission or application change is authorised by this document.**

## Decision and scope

The 5.21 handoff separates five facts: contact/record relationship, invitation delivery and acceptance, identity assurance, future nomination, and effective access. Only an effective `account_access_grants` permission may open Vault content. A nomination, Linked People association, invitation, identity check or named attorney must never create one implicitly. Implement the design as an extension of the existing Personal Vault permission and estate services, with no Platform Admin, Partner Portal or other-owner scope.

This proposal depends on owner approval of decision 13 in `OWNER_DECISIONS_VAULT_ACCESS.md` **before PR 1**. The other open decisions are listed in [QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md). Until resolved, the affected flows remain unavailable; no design mock-up is evidence of a policy decision.

**Critical RLS interaction:** current `linked_grant_allows_asset/record` can include legacy `contact_links` or `record_contacts` for a contact holding an active grant. Merely adding a nomination row is safe; inserting those legacy links or attaching nominated records to an active contact can unintentionally broaden an existing grant. PR 1 must prove nominations and Linked People `record_links` cannot be interpreted as permissions. Any whole-Vault grant needs a dedicated, narrowly reviewed RLS implementation across records, documents, storage and direct-ID reads; setting a client flag or `allowed_sections` alone is insufficient.

## Proposed data model (subject to decision 13)

**Canonical Vault:** `wallets.id` is the Personal Vault FK; `wallets.owner_user_id` is checked against the authenticated owner. All nomination/authority rows also carry `owner_user_id` for explicit tenant constraints and indexing. A row cannot point to a contact, record or document owned by another Vault. Enforce this through composite owner/Vault constraints or checked `SECURITY DEFINER` mutations with fixed `search_path`, restricted `EXECUTE`, and RLS; ordinary clients may not choose a different owner ID.

1. **`vault_nominations`**: UUID PK; `vault_id` FK to `wallets`; `owner_user_id` FK to `auth.users`; `person_id` FK to owner-scoped `contacts`; `kind` (`estate` or `attorney`); `scope` (`whole_vault` or `selected`); `view_level`; `release_condition`; `status` (`active`, `cancelled`, `released`, `ended`); `notify_person`; actor/time columns for create/change/cancel/release; immutable `created_at`, version or `updated_at` for concurrency. Use a partial unique index for one active nomination per `(vault_id, person_id, kind)` while allowing historical rows. Store recipient auth binding separately from contact identity; never equate an email match with verified recipient ownership. `attorney` nomination and `legal_authorities` require an explicit FK/relationship, not status inference.
2. **`vault_nomination_records`**: `nomination_id` FK with controlled cascade, `record_id`, `record_type` or canonical `asset_id`, and `mode` (`include`, `exclude`); unique `(nomination_id, record_type, record_id, mode)`. Check every target is in the same owner Vault and exists, including mixed canonical/legacy record types. `selected` requires at least one `include` and no `exclude`; `whole_vault` allows only `exclude`. Enforce atomically in the owner mutation service, with database safeguards and a unique/consistent type mapping. Selected scope is a fixed set; future records are **not** added. Whole-Vault scope is dynamic over this owner's future Personal Vault assets, less explicitly excluded items and any product/legal exclusions approved by the owner. The existing-record exclusion editor and new-record “Keep out” path must update this table, never contact links.
3. **`legal_authorities`**: UUID PK; owner/Vault/contact FKs; `authority_type`, `registered`, `registration_ref`, `usable_when`, `restrictions_note`, document FK to an owner-owned private attachment, approved `scope_areas`, status (`pending_check`, `confirmed`, `needs_attention`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, `ended`), reviewer/activation/withdrawal actor and timestamps, reason, and version. The authority type defines a maximum scope; the owner may narrow it but cannot widen it. Encrypt sensitive reference/restriction fields using an approved server-side key-management path; no plaintext in client search, logs or recipient projections. Evidence may be recorded while authority is inactive. Do not add the table until the evidence and key-handling design is approved.
4. **Existing `account_access_grants`**, proposed additive `source` (`owner_grant`, `estate_release`, `legal_authority`), `source_id`, `ends_at`, `paused_at` (and reason/actor in append-only audit). Add FK/uniqueness strategy for source references after checking existing unique grant constraints and legacy rows; backfill existing grants as `owner_grant` without changing their effective scope. A future whole-Vault grant type is **unresolved** and needs explicit, comprehensive RLS and storage-policy design; do not encode it as every asset ID or a loose JSON wildcard. Add indexes for `(owner_user_id, linked_user_id, activation_status)`, source lookup, and active expiry. Time expiry must be checked on **every** RLS read, not only by a scheduled job. Revocation/withdrawal likewise must deny subsequent reads immediately.
5. **Invitation/release linkage**: reference existing `contact_invitations` by invitation ID and recipient binding, but keep delivery/acceptance/expiry separate from nomination status. Use existing `death_reports`, `estate_access_claims`, `probate_cases` and append-only `estate_access_decisions`; add a nomination-to-approved-release reference only when its reviewer workflow is approved. Retain one Vault-level estate release decision applied to eligible active nominations; each effective grant still records its exact source and scope. Early-access requests use the existing request/To do mechanism only after authorised type and owner routing are validated.

Suggested indexes: nominations `(owner_user_id, status, created_at DESC)` and `(person_id, status)`; scope `(nomination_id, mode)`; authorities `(owner_user_id, status)` and `(person_id, status)`; reviewer lookup on approved release/source IDs. Prefer `RESTRICT` or soft-retained history for evidence and decisions; do not cascade-delete an audit trail.

## States and effective access

Contact saved → invitation attempted/sent/not delivered/expired/accepted → identity pending/verified → nomination active → release pending → reviewer approved → effective grant is a set of separate transitions. A step can be absent or fail without advancing the next. “Access now: none” is true for nominations and recorded authorities. Recipient-safe projection returns **only nomination kind and state** plus a neutral owner label after identity binding; no scope, IDs, counts, exception count, names of records, values, document title/filename, search results or inventory. No direct table SELECT for nominees. Notifications carry no private metadata.

**Share now:** the owner explicitly selects eligible records (default view only) and confirms. Reuse the current `Give access` scoped payload and grant enforcement, with a reviewed server-side mutation that checks owner, contact binding, Vault lifecycle, each selected record, IDV and recipient activation as applicable. Do not allow a nomination ID or contact relationship to act as an access token. Whole-Vault lifetime sharing and “suggest changes” remain gated by owner decisions and a full RLS review. Expiry/revoke checks live in RLS as well as UI.

**Death release:** existing `createDeathReport`, evidence upload/review, `applyDeathReportAction`, estate claim and `applyProbateCaseAction` provide parts of the pipeline. The nomination release service must require, in a single guarded and idempotent decision path: (1) reported death, (2) accepted evidence, (3) verified death / estate lock, (4) separate authorised Platform Admin release approval with reason and required quorum/policy, plus bound nominee IDV and any required authority/probate condition. Confirmed death or a successful ID check alone never emits a grant. Existing `probate_cases` approval path needs careful compatibility review; it currently can activate a scoped grant, so release design must prevent it becoming an alternate nomination bypass. Dispute, recovery and revocation must fail closed. No automatic release implementation is authorised now.

**Power of Attorney:** record the named attorney and evidence first. Independently verify the person's identity, legal validity/registration and whether authority is usable now under approved jurisdiction rules. Only a Platform Admin reviewer with the proper capability, evidence, reason and policy approval may activate a source-linked grant within the legal type's maximum and owner's narrower scope. Owner withdrawal or expiry immediately invalidates that authority-derived grant without deleting the person or unrelated owner grants. A reviewer must not approve their own request; reuse the existing sensitive-action/quorum controls where policy requires them. Legal requirements remain open in QUESTIONS.md.

## RLS, audit, lifecycle and abuse controls

- Owner-only create/update/cancel/withdraw under `auth.uid() = wallets.owner_user_id` and `lf_vault_allows_owner_mutation`; no recipient mutation, no client-supplied reviewer status. Store changes through constrained server services/RPCs that recheck all FKs and lifecycle state. RLS policies default deny. Reviewer reads/actions require `requireAdminAccess` plus operation-specific capability and case assignment/dual approval where required; Partner Portal has no route or role path.
- Nominees read only a narrow security-definer projection after binding auth user to the accepted invitation/contact; no scope or joins to records. Unknown IDs return the same generic denial as inaccessible IDs. Owner-facing lists use the owner boundary; cross-owner guessing and search must yield no content or counts.
- Existing `audit_events` is append-only. Add event types for nomination create/change/cancel/release/exception, authority record/check/activate/withdraw, access give/change/pause/end/revoke and early requests, with actor, owner, entity IDs, time, policy decision and reason; exclude sensitive values, titles and document text. Record history reads only authorised, redacted events. Preserve previous events after cancellation/deletion subject to approved retention/erasure policy.
- Guard repeated submit/approval with unique source IDs, idempotency key, version/CAS transition and transactionally coupled state/grant/audit writes. Idempotent resend must not issue duplicate invitations or grants. Prevent stale reviewer decisions, self-approval, changed contact binding and cross-tenant confused-deputy requests. Recheck permission on every read, download and signed URL; access termination is immediate even if notification is delayed.
- Do not assume `ON DELETE CASCADE` is acceptable for nomination/audit history. Agree retention, legal hold and erasure rules before implementing deletion; redact or tombstone personal data while preserving lawful event integrity. Apply the same expiry/revocation predicates to every RLS function, storage policy and viewer loader.

1. **Separate dependencies:** Release #93 document-title migration remains independent. Linked People PR 1 migration is on a separate branch and not part of this plan/staging baseline. Review and verify its schema on isolated/local then approved Coolify TEST before any dependent UI. Do not combine either migration with Vault Access.
2. **After decision 13:** create additive PR 1 SQL for nomination/scope/authority tables, owner RLS, recipient-safe projection, reviewer policies, audit events and nullable source fields on existing `account_access_grants`. Include constraints and indexes above; preserve legacy grants. Introduce no grant-creating trigger or automatic release. Run owner/cross-owner/nominee/role tests in rollback-only local Supabase, then separately approve hosted schema migration. The protected app release workflow does not itself apply migrations.
3. Verify exact hosted TEST migration order and schema drift before each application release. The pending Release #93 migration has its own version/order; Linked People PR 1 migration precedes Vault Access PR 1 in the intended implementation branch. If Release #93 lands between them, rebase/reconcile and run conflict checks; never replay/renumber an applied migration. Deploy backward-compatible schema first, then app PRs. New tables stay unreachable until RLS and service gates pass.
4. Forward recovery is the default after a deployed additive schema: disable new feature paths, repair policies/data with reviewed additive migrations, preserve audit and old grants. Local rollback can drop unshipped tables inside a transaction; hosted rollback must be separately approved and must not delete nomination/legal history. Maintain compatibility for users with no contacts, no linked people, existing invitations, pre-existing active grants, locked estates and legacy documents.

## Focused security acceptance plan

Tests must exercise direct REST/RLS, server routes and UI with two owners, nominees, uninvited contacts, reviewers and Partner accounts; use synthetic local data at `127.0.0.1:55421` before separately approved hosted TEST verification. Assert:

1. Cross-owner nomination, authority, scope, document and grant writes/reads are denied, including guessed IDs and altered owner/Vault IDs.
2. A nominee sees only kind/state before release; no private inventory, names, counts, values, documents, filenames, search results, signed URLs or exception counts.
3. Saving a contact, Linked People association, invitation acceptance, ID verification and future nomination each leave `account_access_grants` unchanged; no combination of role labels grants access.
4. Owner-confirmed Share now uses the existing scoped grant path and cannot exceed selected records, documents or owner Vault. Legacy `contact_links`/`record_contacts` cannot widen a nomination-derived or authority-derived grant.
5. Death report, evidence and death confirmation leave nominee access locked. Reviewer release requires every approved predicate, IDV, capability, reason and anti-self-approval; repeats create one effective grant and one decision history.
6. A named attorney cannot self-activate or exceed type/owner scope. Withdrawal, end date, owner revoke, dispute and estate lock deny subsequent record, search and storage reads immediately; unrelated grants persist.
7. Historical append-only audit and record history remain intact and redacted. Admin, Partner and founder launcher boundaries remain isolated. Existing Linked People criteria 1–12 and Vault Access criteria 13–26 are tested per future PR.
