# Legacy Fortress Product Copy Standard

Legacy Fortress speaks like a calm, knowledgeable guide. Customer copy is
friendly, plain, professional, precise, supportive, respectful and
non-judgmental.

## Working Rules

- Ask before assuming. Use `Do you have a Will?`, not `Will missing`.
- Describe the next useful step, not a compliance failure.
- Use the customer's real terms where accuracy matters: Will, executor,
  attorney, beneficiary, probate and death certificate.
- Say what an action does and where it leads. Prefer `Add my Will`,
  `Add executor`, `View invitation` and `Continue setup` to `Open` or
  `Review` when a more exact action exists.
- Keep legal effect precise. A recorded executor is not automatically an
  authorised estate administrator; a linked person does not automatically
  have Vault access.
- Keep personal wishes separate from formal legal dispositions.
- Explain sensitive subjects without urgency, blame or fear.

## Preferred Language

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| There's a little more you could add to this record. | Record incomplete. |
| Bill hasn't accepted your invitation yet. | Invitation pending. |
| Bill is securely linked as an executor. | Role assignment active. |
| Would you like help understanding your options? | You need legal advice. |
| You can invite Bill to securely connect. | Grant access to Bill. |

## Invitations

An invitation should explain who sent it, the relationship being recorded,
what accepting confirms, and that acceptance does not automatically unlock
the owner's living Vault. It must not reveal estate contents or make access,
authority or identity-verification claims that are not true.

## Action Centre

The Action Centre answers `What should I do next?`. Give each item one clear
verb and a canonical destination. The Readiness Engine determines relevance;
copy explains the action and does not determine eligibility.

Use familiar interaction language consistently:

- **Inbox** is the place for actions and follow-up that may need attention.
- **Remind me later** and **Follow up** postpone an action without resolving it.
- A **drawer** reveals why an item is shown and the next available action.
- Use a specific direct verb such as `Add my Will`, `Invite Bill` or `Review
  Barclays` when the destination is known. Avoid generic `Open` or `Review`
  labels when a more useful verb is available.
- A **tracker** shows only factual, authoritative invitation or relationship
  stages. Do not invent delivery or open events.
- **Automatic resolution** means the underlying canonical condition is
  satisfied; opening an item alone does not resolve it.

## Professional Help

Offer general information and an explicit choice to explore help. Do not
present Legacy Fortress as a solicitor, create a commercial lead silently,
or imply that a signal is a legal or tax conclusion.

## Death, Capacity and Access

Use calm, accurate language for death, incapacity, probate and authority.
Never reduce a protected post-death process to a single upload or imply that
an invitation, link or identity check alone grants estate access.
