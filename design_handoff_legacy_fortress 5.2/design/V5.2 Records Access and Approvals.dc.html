AAccessibility and appearance

The standard every V5.2 screen meets, with the fixes from the review: placeholder and caption contrast, 13px minimum text, larger access badges, stronger input borders and badge outlines. Appearance follows the phone by default.

a1Accessibility standard (WCAG 2.2 AA + Apple HIG)
Text sizeMinimum 13px anywhere in the product (dates, chips, captions, tab labels).Body and row titles 15–16px. Inputs 16px so iOS doesn’t zoom.Badges with numbers 12px bold on an 18px pill.Never shrink text to save space; wrap instead.
Large textFollow iOS Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility size.Test at 200% and the largest size: rows grow taller, text wraps, nothing is cut off.Two-column fields stack to one column. Names and amounts never truncate; long file names shorten in the middle with the full name in the accessible label.Web: works at 200% browser zoom and 320px width.
Tap and click targets44 × 44 minimum for every control, avatar and row.Rows 52–64px. Visual circles can be 36px inside a 44px target.At least 8px between separate targets; avatars in a row get 44px slots, no overlap.
Not colour aloneAccess: badge glyph + badge fill + words.Connection: dashed vs no outline + words in the sheet.Family vs professional: circle vs rounded square.Task types: heading icon, tile shape, weight, trailing control.Status chips always carry an icon and a word.
Increase contrast (iOS) / forced colours (web)Card borders and dividers → #8C7F79 (dark #A89A92).Secondary text → #4A3F3A (dark #E0D5CE).Badge and tile outlines 2px.Hover and pressed fills one step stronger.Web forced-colours mode: system colours, outlines kept on all controls.
Motion, focus and readingReduce motion: sheets, panels and toasts fade in 120ms or appear instantly; nothing slides, scales or auto-scrolls.Bold text: all weights +100.Focus-visible ring on every control (2px gap + 2px #8C4A32, dark #F0A98A).Every icon-only control has a label; avatars read name, relationship and status.Toasts stay 6s minimum and are announced; nothing important lives only in a toast.

Record access states and record historyNew 22 Oct

Linked isn’t access. A person appears on a record only if they have a relationship with that record: designated, limited authority, or released. Everyone else stays off it. The badge shows effective access; the avatar outline shows connection. Tapping an avatar opens that person’s access for this record (6m). Every record keeps a permanent history (6n). These describe target behaviour; the UI must not claim enforcement until the backend is verified.
