People & Access V4, record forms and interaction rules

Uses the same type, colours, Material Symbols and components as the redesign. Newest at the top: 5 interaction rules, 4 record forms, 3 mobile, 2 and 1 desktop People & Access. Mary Hale and the record names are sample data.

5Interaction rules: one add control, one hover language

I agree on both points. A big black “Add document” button that silently attaches to the top asset is a mistake; adding should always happen where the user is looking. And the Action Centre hover tint is worth keeping, applied quietly to everything clickable.

4Record forms: required first, optional clearly secondary

What changes from the current forms: required fields sit in a short block at the top, each marked “Required”. Everything else is in collapsible “Optional” groups that start closed. Fields are two columns at most, not three. Dropdown chevrons get 40px of space inside the field so they don’t crowd the edge. There’s one Cancel (the duplicate top-right Cancel is removed), and documents and photos share one attachment area. The same template works for every record type; only the field list changes.

1Desktop: People directory and person panel

Three tabs: People, Requests, Sharing. Each row shows three separate states (Connected, Identity verified, Access granted) as one compact progress strip, so “verified” is never confused with “can see records”. Row actions are round icon buttons: send (only when not invited) and key (Give access). Clicking a row opens one side panel; nothing nests inside it.
