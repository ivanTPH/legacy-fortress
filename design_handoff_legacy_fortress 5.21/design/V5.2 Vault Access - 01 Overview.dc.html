Who can see your Vault, now and later

An owner can nominate someone for their whole Vault or selected records after their death, share selected records now, and record a Power of Attorney. Every one of these is separate from being a contact, being linked to a record, being invited and having your identity checked. Nothing on this board replaces an approved screen: it adds the missing states and joins them up. Where this board and Vault Master V5.2 differ on styling, Vault Master wins. Security and data-protection decisions win over both.

AFive separate facts about every person

Linked People V5.2 tracks four facts (Recorded, Invited, Verified, Authorised). This board splits Authorised into what the owner has arranged for later (Nomination) and what the person can see today (Access), so a nominee is never shown as having access. Each fact changes only when the owner or the release process acts, and none implies another.
