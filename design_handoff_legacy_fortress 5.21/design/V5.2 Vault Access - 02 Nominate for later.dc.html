Step 3 of 4

When can Lucy see it?

Only after all four steps. The same release steps apply to everyone you nominate.

  1. 1Your death is reportedBy your executor or someone you nominated.
  2. 2Evidence is checkedA death certificate, and the grant of probate where it's needed.
  3. 3Your death is verifiedBy the Legacy Fortress review team.
  4. 4The release is approvedLucy is told and can sign in. She can view, not change or delete.
lockReporting a death on its own never opens your Vault.
