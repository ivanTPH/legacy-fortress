LPower of Attorney: recorded by the owner, checked, and only then in effect

Chosen from va-n2 as "Under a Power of Attorney". Different authorities give different powers, so the type sets what the attorney could ever see. Three things are kept apart: the attorney's identity, whether the authority is valid, and whether it can be used now. Access starts only when all three are met and the review team confirms it. UK terms used; legal wording to be confirmed with a solicitor.
