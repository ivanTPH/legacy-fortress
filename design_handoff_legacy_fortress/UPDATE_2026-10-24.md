## What changed
**Navigation and naming**
- "Actions" / "Action Centre" are now **To do** everywhere. The groups are Needs you · Waiting on others · Suggested. The bell = notifications only.
- Mobile header: menu · **My Vault ▾** (vault switcher) · + Add · search · bell. There is no logo inside the app.
- Web sidebar: brand block with a switch chevron at the top. Nav items use outline icons; the selected item gets a white row and an ink tile. Every item opens its own page (Dashboard, Add to my Vault, To do, six areas, People & Access, Settings).

**Home / Dashboard**
- Mobile: a compact dark total card (display only, no arrow), then area cards. No To do panel and no "Your vault" heading.
- Web: a stat strip (Total · Records · People · To do), then the area grid. No To do card.
- A daily To do sheet appears on the first open of the day (mobile).
- Monthly summary email: counts only, normal sign-in link, Monthly / Weekly / Off.

**Content**
- Every content icon tile is filled ink with a white icon. Exceptions: organisation logos, and the Waiting and Suggested task tiles.
- Areas **and** sections use the same card grid (3 columns on web, 2 on mobile). Lists are only for records.
- Empty wording: "No records yet". Right chevron = opens a page; down chevron = expands in place.
- Bank list and record: masked numbers with eye and copy buttons; logos in white tiles.

**New pages (section P, mobile + web)**
- Add to my Vault: choose an area → choose a section → record form.
- Empty section state.
- Settings › Profile (optional photo), Security, Notifications, Support.
- Document added (u1, u2): confirmation toast, document card with View · Edit details · Replace, and "We found in this document" suggestions. Suggestions only, nothing changes until the user chooses, and each choice is written to history.
