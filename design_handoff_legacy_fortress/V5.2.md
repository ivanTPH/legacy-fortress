## Vault Master — 24 Oct
`design/Vault Master V5.2.dc.html` is the single, interactive reference for the Personal Vault on mobile and web, in light and dark. Where it and an older file differ, the master wins. Older Vault files carry a HISTORY banner.
- **Grids, not lists, for areas:** categories (Dashboard / Home) and sub-categories (e.g. Finances › Banks, Pensions) use the same card grid: 3 columns on web, 2 on mobile. Card = ink tile + name + chevron, divider, then "{n} records · £value" or "No records yet · Add first record". Lists are only for records inside a sub-category (Banks list).
- **Add to my Vault:** web sidebar item under Dashboard (plus the round + on the Dashboard); mobile + in the Home header. Flow: choose area (6 cards with examples) → choose section → record form. Only a name is required to save.
- **Sub-page pattern (P section):** back link (mobile) or breadcrumb (web), title, then grouped cards of 56–64px rows with ink icon tiles; settings use switches and radios inside rows. Empty sections show one centred card with the area icon, what to record, and one primary action.
- **Profile photo:** optional. 72px circle with initials until a photo is added; camera badge = "Add a photo". Shown only to connected people. Same avatar is used in the header (web) and on People rows.
- **Adding a document (u1, u2):** after upload, a toast confirms where it went ("Will added to Legal › Wills"), then the record shows the document card: preview thumbnail (page 1), file name, pages, who added it, and View · Edit details · Replace.
- **Suggestions from the document ("We found in this document"):** shown under the document card, one row per finding with its own actions and "Not now". Examples: executors named in a Will (link existing person / add and invite new person), signing date (add to record); a statement whose bank, account or sort code doesn't match the record or any record (Add as new record · Move to a record · Keep here). Rules: suggestions only, nothing changes until the user chooses; numbers shown as "ending 7781"; read inside Legacy Fortress, never shared; "it can make mistakes" note; each choice is written to Record history; a contributor's document only gets suggestions after the owner approves it.
- **Search (qs1–qs3):** mobile = full screen with a focused field; web = 520px dropdown under the top-bar field. Searches record names, people, document titles and To do only; never account numbers or values. Results grouped Records · Documents · People · To do, match highlighted. Before typing: "Try searching for" chips. No results: one card with Add to my Vault. Recent searches are not stored.
- **Menu drawer (qm1, mobile):** profile row with Switch, Vault areas (outline icons), People & Access, Support, Sign out. Web uses the sidebar.
- **Vault switcher (qm2):** Your Vault (check) and Fortresses you're linked to, each with your role and access state (e.g. "You're executor · Locked until release"). Admin and Partner never appear; they're separate sign-ins.
- **Documents (qd1, qd2):** list per record (+ to add; ink tile; who added and when; approved contributor documents say so). Viewer: page preview with zoom, Details · Replace · Delete, added-by row and document history (views, downloads).
- **Add a person (qi1):** name, relationship, email, Family & friends / Professional, "Send invitation now" switch. Note: adding or inviting never shares records.
- **Every sidebar item opens its own page** and becomes the selected item, including To do.
- **Web sidebar:** brand block at the top (logo, "Legacy Fortress", "My Vault", switch chevron) is the only filled tile. Nav items use plain outline icons; the selected item shows a filled ink tile with a white icon on a white row.
- **Every icon tile in content is filled ink with a white line icon:** categories, sub-categories, documents, record history, settings rows, To do tiles. Dark mode: `#EFE3DA` tile, dark icon. Exceptions: organisation logos (white tile) and Waiting / Suggested task tiles (outline, to keep task types distinguishable without colour).
- **One name: "To do".** Replaces "Actions" (tab) and "To do" (page, sidebar, links) everywhere. The To do page has three groups: **Needs you** · **Waiting on others** · **Suggested**. The bell stays as notifications (things that happened).
- **Mobile Home has no To do panel.** To do lives in the bottom tab; its badge = open "Needs you" items. Web Dashboard has no To do card either: To do lives in the sidebar (badge = open items) and the stat strip shows the count. Neither mobile nor web shows a "Your vault" heading (the header/sidebar already says My Vault).
- **Daily To do sheet (mobile):** on the first open of the day, only if there are open items, a bottom sheet over Home lists the top three (titles only) with **Not now** and **Open To do**. Dismissing (button, swipe down, tap outside) hides it for the rest of the day. Never shown twice a day, never over another screen, never on top of a sign-in or security prompt. Reduce motion: appears without sliding. Screen readers: announced as a dialog, focus on the title.
- **Monthly summary email (m3):** once a month, only if there is at least one open item or update. Counts only ("3 things to do", "5 updates since 1 Oct"), a button to the normal sign-in, and Change frequency / Turn off links. Never names, record titles, values, documents or one-tap sign-in links. Settings › Notifications: Monthly (default) · Weekly · Off.
- Home order (mobile): summary (total, updated) → category cards. No "Your vault" heading; 12px gaps on mobile. Mobile summary = option 1a: compact dark card with total and Updated only, display only (no arrow, not tappable). Web Dashboard follows the light layout from the earlier desktop prototype: title and round Add, one stat strip (Total recorded value · Records · People · To do), category cards in three columns (no heading) with a divider and "{n} records · Updated {date}" or "No records yet · Add first record". Icon tiles are filled ink.
- **Chevrons:** right chevron = opens another page; down chevron = expands in place (turns to up when open). Never use a chevron on something that isn't tappable.
- **Empty categories and sections:** "No records yet" (never "Nothing yet" or "0 records"). Counts read "1 record", "3 records".
- **Mobile app header:** menu (left) · "My Vault ▾" title, which is also the vault switcher (opens the list of your Vault and linked Fortresses) · search · notifications. No Legacy Fortress logo inside the signed-in app; the brand shows on the app icon, sign-in, splash and in the menu drawer. Web sidebar keeps the brand.
- **Notifications vs To do:** the bell lists things that happened (invite accepted, document added, new sign-in); it's a record, read or unread, and the badge counts unread events. To do lists things you need to do; items stay until done or dismissed, and the count is open tasks. When an event needs a decision, the notification links to its To do item; it's never counted as a task in the bell.

## Icons, logos and category sub-pages (section S) — decided 23 Oct
- **Category tiles: filled dark tile (s1), now extended to every icon tile (see Vault Master).** 40px, 10px radius, `--lf-task-todo-bg` ink with a white line icon; dark mode `#EFE3DA` with a dark icon.
- **Records use the organisation's logo** wherever one exists (banks, building societies, insurers, pension and investment providers, solicitors, utilities, digital platforms). White tile, 1px `--lf-border-control` hairline, 9px radius (11px at 44px); 36px in lists, 44px on the record page. Show the logo whole; never crop, recolour or stretch it. The same tile style in dark mode (white tile stays white so logos keep their colours).
- **Fallback:** no logo → monogram (first letter, bold, organisation colour if known, else ink) in the same white tile. No organisation (property, possessions) → category line icon on the `--lf-surface-tile` tint.
- Logos come from a vetted, licensed logo source or a bundled set keyed by provider; never fetched from a URL a user types. Decorative: `alt=""`, the row text carries the name.
- **Category list page (s4)**, applies to every category and subcategory: title + "{n} accounts · £total" + round + Add (44px target, ink). One row with the search field and a Sort button. One card of 64px rows: logo, name, "{type} · updated {date}", value, chevron. No status chip on active items; closed items sit behind a "Closed {items} (n)" link at the end. No sensitive numbers on the list.
- **Record page (s5):** back link + Edit pill in the header; logo + name + type; value + As of; **Account details** card with one row per field. Sensitive fields (account number, sort code, policy number, card number, membership number) show masked, with a 44px eye (Show/Hide, `aria-pressed`) and 44px copy button on the right of the row. Auto-hide after 30s; clipboard clears after 60s; toast "Copied". Reveal and copy are audited (no values in the log). Holder name comes from the profile, not free text.

## Web adaptation rules
- Layout: 264px sidebar + 60px top bar (existing V4 desktop shell). Content max 1080px.
- **Record page:** two columns. Main column: Value card (amount, As of, Update value) → Details → Documents. Right rail (380px): People linked to this record → Record history (collapsed disclosure).
- **Sheets become right-hand side panels** (440px, scrim, Close top-right, Esc closes, focus moves into the panel and returns to the trigger).
- **Full-screen forms become centred dialogs** (640px), footer Cancel · primary action right-aligned.
- **Review document:** preview on the left (secure viewer, no download before approval), proposer, proposed change, comments and decision on the right (440px). Reject opens a 560px dialog.
- **Avatars:** 36px inside a 48px target. Hover gives a native tooltip (name · relationship · status) only, no hover cards. Click opens W2.
- **Rows and buttons:** pointer cursor, hover `#F6EFEA`, pressed `#EBE0D8`, focus-visible ring (inset on rows, outset on buttons). Hover never reveals hidden actions.
- Notification bell in the top bar uses the same content as 7a.

## Accessibility standard (applies to every screen, mobile and web)
Target: WCAG 2.2 AA plus Apple Human Interface Guidelines. Many users are older and use phones in dim light.
- **Text size:** minimum 13px anywhere. Body and row titles 15–16px. Inputs 16px. Number badges 12px bold on an 18px pill. Wrap instead of shrinking.
- **Contrast** (measured, light / dark): body 17.6 / 14.3, secondary text 6.2 / 7.8, secondary on page 5.6 / 8.6, links 8.3 / 8.6, primary button 17.0 / 14.4, Pending chip 6.3 / 8.2, Approved 5.7 / 7.4, destructive 6.5 / 8.5, family avatar 7.5 / 7.8, professional avatar 6.7 / 7.6, input border 3.9 / 6.1, focus ring 6.7 / 8.6. Minimums: text 4.5:1, borders and focus 3:1.
- **Fixes applied in V5.2:** placeholder and caption grey `#8C7F79` → `#6B5F5A`; all 11–12px text → 13px; tab and count badges 10px → 12px; access badges 18px → 20px with 13px glyph; input borders `#D9CFC8` → `#8C7F79`; lock-badge and Waiting/Suggested tile outlines → `#8C7F79`.
- **Large text:** support iOS Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility size; test at 200% and largest. Rows grow, text wraps, two-column fields stack, names and amounts never truncate (file names truncate in the middle; full name in accessible label). Web: 200% zoom and 320px width.
- **Targets:** 44 × 44 minimum; 8px between separate targets; avatars never overlap.
- **Not colour alone:** access (glyph + fill + words), connection (dashed outline + words), family vs professional (shape), task types (icon, shape, weight, control), chips (icon + word).
- **Increase contrast (iOS) / forced colours (web):** borders and dividers `#8C7F79` (dark `#A89A92`), secondary text `#4A3F3A` (dark `#E0D5CE`), badge and tile outlines 2px, hover and pressed one step stronger.
- **Bold text:** all weights +100.
- **Reduce motion:** sheets, panels and toasts fade in 120ms or appear instantly; nothing slides, scales or auto-scrolls.
- **Screen readers and focus:** every icon-only control labelled; avatars read name, relationship and status; task rows read their type first; disclosure uses `aria-expanded`; dialogs trap focus and return it. Toasts ≥ 6s, announced, never the only place information appears.

## Appearance and dark mode
- **Settings › Appearance (A2):** Theme = Automatic (default, follows iPhone / OS) · Light · Dark, saved per device. Text size, Bold text, Increase contrast and Reduce motion follow the phone and are shown as read-only "From iPhone" rows with "Open iPhone display settings".
- Web follows `prefers-color-scheme`, `prefers-contrast`, `prefers-reduced-motion` and `forced-colors`, with the same Theme setting overriding.
- **Dark tokens:** page `#171210`, card `#241C19`, sidebar `#1C1613`, divider `#2F2622`, border `#3A302B`, button outline `#4D413B`, input border `#A89A92`; text `#F3ECE7`, secondary `#BCAEA6`, link `#F0A98A`; primary button `#EFE3DA` with `#1B1411` text; hover `#30261F`, pressed `#3B2F28`; focus ring `#F0A98A`; family avatar `#4A2E22`/`#F4C3AD`; professional `#173D3A`/`#9FD9D2`; Pending `#3D2E0F`/`#F3C56B`; Approved `#183A26`/`#8FD6A8`; Rejected / destructive `#F2A59F`. Released badge becomes light fill with dark glyph; locked and limited badges keep their outlines.
- UAT strip stays red with white text in both themes.
- Never pure black backgrounds; no colour-only differences introduced in dark.
- **Implement from `tokens.css` (web) and `tokens.json` (any platform).** Every colour has light, dark, light-high-contrast and dark-high-contrast values; type, target, layout and motion sizes are included. Components use `var(--lf-…)` only; no hard-coded hex. `<html data-theme="light|dark">` for the Light/Dark choice; no attribute = Automatic.

- [ ] Web at 200% zoom and 320px width: no horizontal scrolling except the document preview.

- [ ] Reduce motion on: no sliding or scaling anywhere.
