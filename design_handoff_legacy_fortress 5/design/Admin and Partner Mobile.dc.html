1Platform Admin on mobile · Super admin

Each phone is the live Admin Console at 390 × 844, so everything is tappable: tabs, rows, sheets, the role picker and the confirm-with-reason dialog. Sign-in is a two-step flow. The bottom tabs are Overview, Verification and Audit, plus Menu for every other section. Table rows become two-line list rows, and selecting one opens a full-screen sheet.
