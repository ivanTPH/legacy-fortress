Changing or removing a record

One way in: Edit in the record header (6b). The form opens with the value first, then every detail including the section, and ends with the option to close or delete. Documents and access stay on the record page, where they’re already visible. A closed account is usually marked as closed rather than deleted, because executors may still need to know it existed.
